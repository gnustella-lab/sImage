02 — Features

Fewer traces.
More control.

Every tool reduces data exposure without complicating your workflow. No account, no cloud, no processing queue.

01

Metadata cleaning

Re-encodes the image to remove EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, comments, and other attached data blocks. Then verifies the result.

Always on
02

Selective pixelation

Mark only sensitive areas such as faces, license plates, addresses, or document numbers.

03

Blur and solid cover

Choose adjustable blur or a solid cover for information that must stay hidden.

04

Resize

Reduce dimensions while preserving aspect ratio, or create a custom output for its destination.

05

Smart compression

Adjust JPEG and WebP quality to share smaller files without losing what matters.

06

Private conversion

Convert between JPEG, PNG, and WebP in your browser without relying on third-party websites.

03 — Architecture

Your browser is the server.

sImage is a static page. Once loaded, every operation happens inside your browser — the image never travels over the internet.
